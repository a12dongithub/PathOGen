% \subsection{What the three tables establish}

% FID and KID establish distributional similarity but cannot demonstrate condition use. Spatial correlations establish map adherence but can be inflated by an in-loop analyzer. The independent rows reduce that concern, and the random-real-tile row shows that realistic tissue alone is insufficient. Finally, within-image dose response demonstrates that the FiLM controls alter the requested measurement in an ordered manner. Together, the evaluations support using generated pairs as controlled perturbations while still requiring per-sample verification for downstream interpretation.
